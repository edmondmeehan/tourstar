THE NAME

  • Write it TourStar: one word, capital T and capital S.
  • Not "Tour Star", "Tourstar" or "TOURSTAR" in body text. All caps is fine in pixel-font headlines.
  • The company is TourStar, LLC. Prizes are fulfilled by Please & Thank You.
  • The website is tourstarapp.com (lowercase, no "www").

LOGO

The dashed line shows the minimum clear space: keep other text and graphics at least the height of the star away from the logo.

Do
  • Use the file provided, on a dark background (Night or Stage purple below) or a dark, simple part of a photo.
  • Keep it at least 120 px wide on screens.
  • Scale it evenly.
Don't
  • Place it on white or light backgrounds. The lettering is white.
  • Recolor, stretch, rotate, outline or add shadows to it.
  • Retype the name in another font, or swap the gold star for another shape.
  • Put it on busy images where it's hard to read.

The current logo file is 737 px wide, which is right for screens. For print or large signage, ask for the high-resolution version.

Body text: any clean sans-serif (IBM Plex Sans, Inter or the system font).

All three fonts are free from Google Fonts.

Do
  • Scale pixel art up in whole steps (2×, 3×, 4×) with "nearest neighbor" so edges stay crisp.
  • Show the group together, or rotate through all eight across a campaign.
Don't
  • Blur, smooth, redraw or trace the characters.
  • Change their skin tones or features, or give them a real person's name.
  • Put them in content about alcohol, drugs, gambling, politics or anything a teenage player shouldn't see.

VOICE AND MESSAGING

Talk like someone who has worked a tour: confident, funny and specific. Short sentences. Music words over gaming words ("show", "setlist", "encore", not "level up your gameplay").

Approved lines

  • From Mom's basement to global superstar.
  • You don't watch the show. You play it.
  • Play every show.
  • Every legend starts in Mom's basement.
  • Fame in the game. Prizes in real life.
  • Your first gig is an open mic. Get on the list.
  • Seven levels. One shot at legend.

Say it right

  • Before launch: "Join the waitlist" or "Coming to iPhone". Don't say "Download now" or use the App Store badge until the app is live; after launch, use Apple's official "Download on the App Store" badge exactly as Apple supplies it.
  • Hashtags: #TourStar and #PlayEveryShow.
  • Don't name or tag real artists, labels or venues unless there's a signed partnership in place.

PROMOTIONS AND PRIZES

  • Prizes are won with points earned by playing. Points can't be bought; never suggest that spending money helps you win.
  • Don't call it gambling, betting or a lottery, and don't promise "cash prizes".
  • Only name a specific prize (a meet & greet, tickets, merch) when that reward is live in the game or covered by official rules for that promotion.
  • Any giveaway run on social media needs written rules approved before it's posted.
