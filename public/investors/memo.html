EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

TourStar is a music career game for iPhone where players take an artist from Mom's basement to global superstar and play every show themselves. It pairs a rhythm game with a full career simulation, and it gives real prizes, including VIP experiences and merch fulfilled by Please & Thank You, to the players who earn them.

The game was first released on iOS in 2015, built in Unity. In 2026 it was rebuilt from scratch with cloud saves, real AI-recorded songs, weekly leagues, a licensed-artist platform and a live-operations admin. The new version is in TestFlight ahead of its App Store launch.

The company is raising capital to launch and market the new game, bring it to Android, grow its roster of artist partners and build player-run labels and a ticketing marketplace.

THE COMPANY

TourStar, LLC develops and operates TourStar and related music-and-gaming products. It was formed by Music Players Group and ground(ctrl) to develop and promote music-industry technology, with TourStar as its first product.

  • Music Players Group was formed by music industry professionals to merge social gaming and music, build new fan bases for recording artists and create brand integrations inside games. Its leadership has worked with Lil Wayne, Nicki Minaj, The Roots, Jill Scott, Meek Mill and Keri Hilson.
  • ground(ctrl) was a fan services company that partnered with major artists, including Linkin Park, Taylor Swift and Nicki Minaj, on technology, products and events for their most dedicated fans.
  • Please and Thank You, Inc., founded by Eddie Meehan, fulfills TourStar's rewards and prizes.

THE GAME

Every decision in a music career shows up in TourStar: whether to spend on a better crew, sign with a label, upgrade the tour bus or give fans new music. Each choice has upsides and consequences.

Play every showNotes fall in time with the player's songs. Their grade sets the payout and fame. Setlists, fan requests and encores matter.
Make musicMixtapes to albums, with optional real AI-recorded songs that earn royalties and can be released to streaming services in the monthly TourStar compilation.
Run the careerLabels, crew and gear, plus thousands of scenarios: label feuds, viral moments, scams and bad nights, with jail and rehab as real setbacks.
Compete togetherWeekly leagues of real players, friends and a shared festival bill, leaderboards, achievements and a Hall of Fame.
Learn the drumsDrum School lessons on real grooves; passing them improves every show.
Win real prizesPoints earned by playing buy real merch and VIP experiences. Points can never be bought.

ARTISTS AND PARTNERS

The 2015 plan called for a select group of artists with profit participation and integrated marketing for their releases and tours. The new game turns that into a platform:

  • Artist packs sold for Gold, with optional merch discounts and presale access.
  • Hosted corners, such as Questlove's Drum School corner, with the artist's tips, bio and sign-off.
  • Featured venues with real ticket links, sponsored stages and tracked taps.
  • Partner statements showing sales, views and plays, with every feature switching off automatically when a license ends.

MARKETING AND PRESS

The goal is to build TourStar into a recognizable music-and-gaming brand that can extend beyond the game.

  • Artists as the channel: partner artists promote their packs, corners and presales to their own fans.
  • Built-in sharing: share cards for S grades, sellouts, level-ups and league promotions; friend invites with referral rewards.
  • Waitlist and launch: tourstarapp.com collects early-access signups with a playable demo.
  • Press and speaking: panels and keynotes at industry events such as SXSW, MIDEM and the New Music Seminar, and talks at university music business programs, with press built around each appearance.
  • Social: artist posts about TourStar, content and contests, and in-game events tied to real holidays and tours.

KEY INVESTMENT HIGHLIGHTS

  • A rebuilt, launch-ready game joining music and gaming, with a decade of design behind it.
  • Founders with long track records with top artists, and an artist partner in Questlove.
  • Real-world prizes from an operator that already runs VIP programs for major touring artists.
  • Several revenue streams: subscription, in-app purchases, merch and partnerships.
  • A partner platform that lets artists, venues and brands take part without building anything.
  • Built as a live service, with leagues, events, alerts and analytics run from one admin.

RISK FACTORS

An investment in TourStar is speculative and could be lost entirely. Key risks include:

  • Market: mobile games are crowded, and player acquisition costs can rise.
  • Execution: the company may not launch, grow or monetize the game as planned.
  • Platforms: TourStar depends on Apple and Google's rules, fees and approval.
  • Partners: artist and brand features depend on licenses that can end.
  • Prizes and promotions: rewards and any sweepstakes must follow state and federal rules.
  • Liquidity: the securities would be restricted, with no public market.
