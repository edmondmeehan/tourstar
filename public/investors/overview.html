COMPANY OVERVIEW · OCTOBER 2026

TourStar

TourStar is a music career game for iPhone where you play every show. Players take an artist from Mom's basement to sold-out stadiums, and the best players win real VIP experiences and merch fulfilled by Please & Thank You.

This is a refresh of the 2015 TourStar investment memorandum. It describes the game as it exists today, what changed since the first version, and where it goes next.

THE GAME

Every show is playable. Notes fall in time with the player's own songs; they tap to the beat, and their grade decides the payout, fame and the crowd's reaction. Around the shows sits a full career:

  • Tour: seven stage sizes from open mic to stadium encore, with setlists, fan requests, encores and difficulty levels.
  • Make music: mixtapes to albums, each able to carry a real AI-recorded song. Releases earn royalties while players are away.
  • Run the business: sign with labels, hire crew and gear, handle scandals, temptations and scams with real consequences (jail, rehab, lost credibility).
  • Skill: Drum School teaches real grooves on a kick, snare and hi-hat; passing lessons improves every show.
  • Compete: weekly leagues of about 30 real players across six divisions, friends and a shared "festival bill", leaderboards and a Hall of Fame for retired Legends.
  • Come back daily: quests, streaks, expiring gig offers from promoters, limited-time tour events and seasonal looks.
  • The Press: AI-written news about each player's career, reviews of their releases and a manager who gives advice based on their real numbers.

WHAT MAKES IT DIFFERENT

  • Real prizes from a real operator. Please & Thank You runs fan clubs and VIP programs for touring artists, so prizes are actual experiences and merch, not coupons.
  • Artists can take part without building anything. A pack, a corner or a venue is set up in the admin in minutes and ends automatically with the license.
  • AI where it adds something. Players' songs are real recordings, and the press covers their actual career.
  • Built to run as a live service. Weekly leagues, events, seasonal looks, push alerts and win-back emails keep players returning, and the admin shows where new players drop off.
