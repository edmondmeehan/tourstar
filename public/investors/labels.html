GAME DESIGN · NEXT FEATURE

Labels

Record labels raise what a player earns on tour, in exchange for a cut. Today players sign with one of four built-in labels. Next, players will start and run their own labels with friends, and labels will compete on a weekly leaderboard.

Updated from the 2015 Labels design (v4) and its focus-group notes, adapted to the game as it exists now: weekly leagues, friends and real-time weeks instead of in-game months.

Starting a label

  • Any player at level 3 or above can found a label: pick a name (checked by the language filter) and one of a set of label logos.
  • Founding costs game cash (proposed: $250,000, scaled to level) and gives a one-time boost: +20 credibility, +5% fame, +5 hype.
  • The founder earns a larger share of the label's bonus than members, which the focus group asked for.
  • Membership type is set at founding: Open (anyone meeting an optional minimum level joins automatically), Invite-only (only the founder invites) or Crew (any member can invite).
  • Only the founder can remove members. Founders can hand the label to another member before leaving.

Joining and being a member

  • Players join through invites from friends (using today's friend codes) or by applying to open labels.
  • Indie labels always take 10% of show earnings. In return members get +20 credibility on signing and +10 on each release.
  • Players stay on one label at a time, player-run or built-in.

Label success and the weekly bonus

Each week, alongside the leagues, every label gets a success score: the fame its members earned that week. A label's multiplier compares it with the average label:

Label bonus = 25% × (this label's weekly fame ÷ average weekly fame of all labels), capped at +50%, applied to members' show pay the following week.

Members get a notification when the bonus changes. Labels in the top 10% at the end of a season win a fame award and a badge.

Label leaderboard

Built-in and player-run labels rank together by weekly success. Tapping a label shows its members, their levels and their fame this week, which doubles as recruiting.

Big labels with standards

  • Velvet Crown and Megaphone Global require a minimum credibility, without showing the exact number: "We don't sign acts we consider a risk."
  • Players who fall below it get a weekly warning. After four weeks they're dropped, with a further −10 credibility and a news story about it.

Leaving

  • Buying out costs 10% of current cash.
  • Credibility drops by 1 for each label the player has ever left ("fans are confused").
  • A founder who leaves takes −25 credibility; the label lives on and they can rejoin later as a member.

WHY IT MATTERS

  • Gives friends a reason to play together every week, which drives retention.
  • Creates player-owned brands people recruit for and share outside the game.
  • Opens sponsorship: a real label or artist can run an official label inside the game.

OPEN QUESTIONS

  • Founding cost and the level needed to start a label.
  • How many logos to offer, and whether VIP or Gold unlocks custom ones.
  • Whether founders can pay a signing bonus to recruits from their own cash.
  • Season length for the label awards (proposed: quarterly).
